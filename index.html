What is a Jyotirlinga?

Jyoti means radiance and linga means sign. A Jyotirlinga is a shrine where Shiva is believed to have manifested as light, beyond form and beginning.

The Shiva Purana tells how Brahma and Vishnu argued over who was greater. A blazing column of fire rose before them with no visible end. Brahma flew up as a swan and Vishnu dug down as a boar. Neither found the limit; Brahma falsely claimed he had, and Shiva revealed himself, cursing Brahma and humbling both gods.

That column is the source of the twelve. The Shiva Purana lists them, and Adi Shankaracharya's hymn made the list famous across India.

Dwadasha Jyotirlinga Stotram

Attributed to Adi Shankaracharya. Transliteration is shown below.

Tradition says those who recite this morning and evening are freed from sins of seven births. Another famous line to chant: Om Tryambakam yajamahe sugandhim pushtivardhanam, urvarukamiva bandhanan mrityor mukshiya maamritat. This is the Mahamrityunjaya mantra from the Rig Veda.
